Quick FAQ

Cannot see a course in my Teacher/Student view?

Contact Support via email (contact.naphonkrit@gmail.com) or IG (@sileiabeans) so Admin can assign instructor permissions or enable course visibility.

How do I resubmit an assignment after grading?

Once graded, submissions lock automatically. Ask your instructor to set the status to "Revision Requested" to unlock resubmission.
